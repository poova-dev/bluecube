## 🏛️ Practice Overview

Blue Cube Architects is a two-decade-old premier Architecture and Interior Design firm headquartered in Chennai. Headed by Principal Architect Mr. G. Harish along with a dedicated team of specialist designers, Blue Cube creates unique, solution-oriented bespoke spaces that amalgamate functionality, aesthetic elegance, and client-centric vision.

- **Philosophy:** *"Everything you can imagine is real"*
- **Core Disciplines:**
  - **Architecture:** Bespoke Residences, Commercial Showrooms, IT Campuses, Institutional & Healthcare facilities
  - **Interior Design:** Aesthetic Clinics & Healthcare, Executive Corporate Suites, Luxury Residences, Boutiques
  - **Container Architecture:** Rapid deployment modular shipping container structures and eco-pods
