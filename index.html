Studio Profile

Blue Cube Architects

Established at the wake of the Millennium, Blue Cube is a two-decade-old architecture firm with its headquarters in Chennai. It is headed by Principal Architect Mr. G. Harish along with a team of talented designers with an eye for detail.

At Blue Cube, we believe that the evolution of a good design is as important as the final product itself. We create unique designs that are an amalgamation of solution-oriented bespoke styles catering to specific client needs. This is made possible through our philosophy of being client-centric in our design approach.

Why Bluecube • Methodology

The Flow

We believe in the concept of flow. Flow in design and flow in the process. Therefore, we consist of 6 individual teams, each consisting of experts in their respective fields:

01

Drafting

Precision construction drawings, dimensional clarity, and comprehensive working schematics.

02

Design

Contextual spatial ideation, client-specific aesthetic language, and material curation.

03

Development

Facility studies, structural engineering alignment, and MEP service integration.

04

Execution

On-ground site engineers enforcing adherence to technical specifications and structural integrity.

05

3D Visualization

Photorealistic computer rendering, spatial lighting studies, and virtual representations.

06

Quality Control

Rigorous audit checkpoints to ensure finished craftsmanship meets rigorous architectural standards.

In-House Expertise with a Single POC

With every specialist from 3D visualizers to lighting designers working in-house, our teams collaborate in real time. This eliminates delays, ensures accountability, and guarantees a cohesive vision from start to finish all coordinated through a single point of contact.

On-Site Quality Management

Our site engineers ensure that the contractor adheres to the specification and meets the implementation criteria. We provide our clients with periodic meeting updates to keep them informed.
